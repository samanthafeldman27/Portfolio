About

Samantha Feldman

I'm a mechanical engineer focused on designing and building reliable hardware that performs in real-world conditions. My experience spans mechanical design, structural analysis, testing, manufacturing, and data-driven problem solving across both academic research and industry.

I'm drawn to hands-on engineering where I can take a problem from concept through design, analysis, prototyping, manufacturing, and testing. I enjoy working at the intersection of design and manufacturing—turning engineered concepts into physical devices while understanding how hardware behaves, fails, and can be improved. I'm particularly interested in R&D, advanced manufacturing, test engineering, and reliability engineering roles where I can contribute across the hardware development lifecycle.

My work includes structural design for competition aircraft, bio-inspired robotics, vibration and dynamical-systems research, precision mechanical design, and process improvement in industry. I've also supported structural analysis and FEA for aerospace engine components at GE Aerospace, though details of that work are limited by confidentiality. These projects reflect my interest in building hardware that is both well-engineered and robust in operation.
