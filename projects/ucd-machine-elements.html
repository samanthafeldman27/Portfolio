UCD Machine Elements Projects

2025 Design Engineer

Coursework at UCD (University College Dublin), module MEEN30300, on designing and sizing fundamental machine elements: gears, shafts, bearings, and precision optical mounts.

My final project (shown above) was a linear-motion gearbox that took rotational motion from a motor and converted it into linear motion driving a rack. I designed the gear train's reduction, sizing each gear pair for the mechanical advantage needed to turn a small motor input into enough torque and the right output speed to drive the rack, then modeled and animated the full assembly in SolidWorks.
