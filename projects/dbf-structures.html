WashU Design/Build/Fly, Structures

2024–2025 Structures Team Lead

WashU's Design/Build/Fly team built a radio-controlled aircraft each year for the AIAA competition. That year's plane, Ursa Major, carried external fuel tanks and an autonomous glider while meeting a strict wingspan limit. As Structures Team Lead, I CADed the entire aircraft, built the wing based on the angles and airfoils the aerodynamics team provided, and designed the wing attachment box, the glider release and pylon systems, and the fuselage, then ran the FEA that validated the aircraft against its flight and ground loads.

The team placed 35th of 117 teams at the 2025 fly-off.
