Medtronic, DRM Green Belt Project

2026 R&D Engineering Intern, DRM Team

My Design for Reliability & Manufacturing (DRM) Green Belt project focused on standardizing how historical DRM reporting data was compared across quarters. The existing process relied on static exports that were overwritten each reporting period, making it difficult to track changes over time and efficiently identify trends.

I applied three core DRM practices, Voice of the Customer, Requirements Flow, and Concept Engineering, to develop a more structured approach for comparing and managing historical data. As part of the Green Belt certification, I also developed a broader understanding of all ten DRM practices and used that foundation to establish a Robust Design, Design for Reliability, and Control methodology to refine requirements, support adoption, and enable continuous improvement.

The project resulted in an architecture for more efficient, data-driven comparisons and decision-making, providing organizational leaders with a more effective way to evaluate historical performance and inform strategic decisions.

Underlying project data and system architecture are internal to Medtronic and redacted above. Happy to talk through the process and my role in more depth in an interview.
