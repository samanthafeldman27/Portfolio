WashU Design/Build/Fly, Co-President

2025–2026 Administrative President

After a year leading the Structures team, I stepped into a club-wide leadership role for the 2025–2026 season, serving as Administrative President alongside a Technical President. I oversaw the administrative and operational side of a 60+ member team, including budgeting, sponsorships, scheduling, competition logistics, and external partnerships, while my counterpart led the six technical sub-teams. Together, we established the team's organizational structure, project timeline, and outreach strategy for the season.

I also coordinated the team's three test flights, monitoring wind conditions, organizing test pilots, and managing the logistics of transporting the aircraft to and from the field. Throughout the season, I worked closely with sub-team leadership to keep projects moving, resolve organizational challenges, and ensure the technical teams had the support and resources they needed.

For our industry outreach, I developed and presented a collaboration proposal to Boeing leadership outlining opportunities for team mentorship, facility tours, and professional development events.

At the 2026 fly-off, the team won the Stan Powell Award for Lessons Learned, one of only five awards AIAA presents at DBF each year, alongside 1st through 3rd place and Best Design Report. Read more in WashU Engineering's writeup.
