UCD Dynamical Systems & Risk Lab

2025 Research Assistant

Vortex-induced vibration (VIV) drives fatigue and, occasionally, failure in bridges, risers, and wind turbines. Working with Dr. Vikram Pakrashi's lab at UCD (University College Dublin), I built a physical rig, a 3D-printed cylinder oscillating on nylon thread in a wind tunnel, to study how VIV amplitude changed with reduced velocity, and how a solid-body impact distorted that motion.

The impact tests reproduced a distinctive flattened, distorted oscillation signature that faded as the impact point moved further away, matching results the lab had previously seen in electrical vibro-impact experiments.
